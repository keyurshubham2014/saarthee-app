From one complaint tracker to Amdavad's civic home

Four research tracks (AMC itself, civic apps worldwide and in India, corporators and alerts, and design) point to the same shift. Saarthee should be the place where an Amdavadi reports anything, follows it to a real fix, reaches their four corporators, hears about water cuts and road closures first, and finds AMC services and drives. Filing with AMC's CCRS becomes optional, not the only path.

There is no public status API, no alerts feed and no current machine-readable corporator list. Citizens' top complaints: monsoon potholes (2,028 this season), waterlogging, encroachment (over 50% negative CCRS feedback), stray cattle, and complaints "closed" with photos from a different place.

Home is a ward feed

Alerts, nearby issues, drives, corporator updates and service tips, scoped to your ward ("your pol"). Every card can be shared to WhatsApp as an image.

Report is one sheet, not six screens

Big icon grid, camera, auto location and ward, optional voice note. Before sending, it shows "3 neighbours already reported this. Add your Me too?"

Fixed means verified

An issue only counts as fixed when the reporter or a neighbour confirms it with a geotagged photo. You get 7 days to reopen, counted from when it was marked fixed.

Your ward, your people

"My pol" shows your 4 corporators and MLA, your ward's scorecard, the things you follow, and the alerts you subscribe to.

Corporators and alerts

How the representative layer stays fair

Data, carefully

  • Build the 2026–31 roster by hand from State Election Commission results and MyNeta affidavits; no official list exists yet.
  • Show only office or consented public numbers. Never the home addresses and personal mobiles in AMC's old PDFs.
  • Four corporators per ward, so the model is many-to-one from day one.

Neutral by design

  • No party logos or colours; same features for every corporator.
  • Corporators can acknowledge and comment. Only the reporter or verified proof closes an issue.
  • An "election mode" switch freezes corporator posts and work-done stats while a Model Code of Conduct applies.

Alerts we can trust

  • No official AMC feed exists, so alerts are curated from AMC press notes, @AmdavadAMC, traffic police, IMD and NDMA.
  • Labelled "Source: AMC (link) · relayed by Saarthee". Never the AMC logo.
  • Push first (free), WhatsApp later for opt-ins, SMS only for critical alerts (needs TRAI DLT registration).

Design direction

"Pol Sunset": warm clay, leaf and marigold

Indigo goes. The new look borrows from a Khadia pol in late-afternoon light: terracotta walls, neem leaves, marigold torans, the Sidi Saiyyed jali as a faint texture, and the Uttarayan kite as the Report button. Fonts are Baloo Bhai 2 for headings and Mukta Vaani for text, both open-licence with full Gujarati and Devanagari. This page is set in them.

Suggested sequence

Roadmap

Weeks 1–4

New look + standalone reporting. Pol Sunset redesign, language picker, 14 categories, ward detection from AMC's ward map, duplicate check and Me too, issue lifecycle with neighbour verification, optional CCRS hand-off. The current reminder and verify loop is reused.

Weeks 5–8

My ward + alerts. Hand-verified corporator, MLA and MP directory, message relay, alerts admin with sources and two-person approval, push by ward and category, services directory, ward map view.

Weeks 9–12

Accountability + community. Corporator claim flow and dashboard, public ward scorecards, escalation ladder, drives with RSVP, RWA pages, IMD and SACHET draft alerts.

Later

Scale channels. WhatsApp reporting and alerts, SMS for critical alerts, ward polls, open-data export, and a paid analytics tier for AMC or CSR partners.
