Saarthee mark · explorations

Seven candidates for the DS §1 mark (rounded square, radius 28%, primary #14674A, white route rising to the upper right, sunrise #C24A1F dot). Each row shows real sizes 16 to 256 px, a 16 px raster zoomed so the pixel grid is visible, the single-colour glyph as an Android status-bar icon (24 dp, zoomed), and a circular launcher mask. The chosen variant is E; see README.md.

On light

A · Preview routeFounder preview geometry (48 grid scaled to 100): three-turn route, thin stroke, white-ringed dot.
B · Bold routeSame story, heavier: stroke 11, flatter dip, dot detached by a clear gap. No ring.
C · Curved roadSmooth S-curve road rising to the dot. Softer, but reads as a wave or a graph at small sizes.
D · Refined route (rejected: reads as trending_up)B weight (stroke 11), dot placed on the line of the last segment with a 3.6-unit natural gap, whole glyph optically centred (bbox 15–85 × 22.5–77.5). Separates cleanly in one colour without masks.
E · Road turn (chosen)Navigation turn: a road rises from the bottom-left, turns right on a generous 18-unit corner and runs on to the dot (the destination) across a 6-unit gap. Glyph bbox 18–83 × 22.5–79, optically centred.
F · Chevron aheadOne bold rounded chevron pointing up-right, the dot just ahead of its tip.
G · Winding laneA lane with two soft curves at an even stroke, climbing to the dot.
