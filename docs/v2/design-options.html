Four design directions for Saarthee

The same three screens in four directions: Home, Report step 1 and an issue that was just fixed. Each direction has its own colours, typeface pairing (Gujarati and English), spacing and animation style. Press "Play motion" on any direction to see how it moves. None of them use AMC's seal or colours, and all keep text readable and touch targets large.

My recommendation is B · Neem. It feels like a neighbourhood app rather than a government form, green ties in with clean streets and tree drives, and the warm orange button makes "Report" impossible to miss. A is the safe choice if you want it to feel official. You can also mix them, for example B's colours with C's bolder motion.
