Civic Blue: the professional redesign

One restrained blue for actions, calm slate neutrals, flat white surfaces with thin borders, and Noto Sans in Gujarati and English. It reads as a serious public-service app, like the best government apps, while staying clearly independent: no AMC seal, no AMC colours, and an independence line wherever AMC information appears.

One colour, one job

Blue means "you can act here". Everything else is neutral, so actions are never hard to find.

Words with every icon

Every tab, status and alert has a label in the user's language. Colour is never the only signal.

Three steps to report

What is it, photo and place, then check and submit. Large tiles, camera first, location filled in.

Independent and sourced

AMC information always shows where it came from. The app never claims to be AMC.

What we deliberately avoid

AMC's identity

No seal or circular emblem, no bronze or amber, no navy gradient, no teal header, no stacked English-over-Gujarati lockup, no "AMC" in the app name.

Playful decoration

No mascots, clip art, confetti or heritage illustrations. Empty states use a single line icon and one action.

Hidden meaning

No colour-only status, no unlabeled icons, no text under 12 sp, no ALL CAPS in Gujarati.
