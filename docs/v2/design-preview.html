Neem: how Saarthee looks, reads and moves

Neem is named for the tree that shades Amdavad's streets. A deep neem green carries the brand, the header and every ordinary action. One warm sunrise orange is kept for a single job: reporting a problem. Rounded Baloo Bhai 2 headings sit on plain Mukta Vaani text, both from Ek Type and designed for Gujarati and English together. Cards are generous and rounded, every status says its name in words, and motion only explains what changed. This page is the visual reference for design system v2.2.

Key screens

Eight screens in the app's light theme, at about 360 dp. Press Play to replay the DS §6 motion: staggered rise, tile pop, progress growth, the Fixed to Verified chip change, the toast with a drawn check and the one-time Report card pulse.

Your device asks for reduced motion, so Play applies each change instantly, as the app will.

Colour

Light theme tokens from DS §2. Contrast is WCAG 2.2, (L1 + 0.05) / (L2 + 0.05). Sunrise is for the report action only and never sits next to error red.

Type

Baloo Bhai 2 for display, headings and numbers (600/700, 800 for display numbers only). Mukta Vaani for body, labels, buttons and metadata (400/500/600). Both by Ek Type, OFL. Baloo never goes below 16 sp; nothing goes below 12 sp; no letter-spacing or capitals on Gujarati.

Elevation: cards use a 1 px border plus a 1 dp shadow (#17251E at 5%); the Report card has a sunrise glow (0 10 22 −10 at 70%); sheets, dialogs and toasts sit at elevation 3. Icons are Material Symbols Rounded, weight 400, 24 dp, filled only for the selected tab and the Report plus.

Motion tokens

DS §6, SaartheeMotion in lib/core/theme/motion.dart. A gentle spring: quick to start, a slight overshoot, a soft settle. Only transform, opacity and colour animate. With reduced motion every change is instant or a cross-fade of 100 ms or less.

What we avoid

do_not_disturb_onAMC's identity

No seal, circle emblem, architecture or wheel. No bronze #9E6B22, amber, navy gradient or teal. No stacked English-over-Gujarati lockup, and never "AMC" in the app name, icon or store title.

do_not_disturb_onPlayful decoration

No mascots, clip art, illustrations or confetti. No Lottie or Rive, nothing that loops except a skeleton while it waits. Empty states are one icon, one line and one action.

do_not_disturb_onHidden meaning

No colour-only status, no unlabelled icons, no text under 12 sp, no capitals or letter-spacing on Gujarati, and no motion that carries meaning without the text changing too.
